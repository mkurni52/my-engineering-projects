Machine Learning for Protein Engineering: MIF-ST Evaluation

Classification: Individual Graduate Project • Target Structure: 7B3E Protein Nanobody Matrix

Project Overview

This independent graduate project evaluated transformer-based Masked Inverse Folding (MIF-ST) architectures to accelerate targeted molecular design and engineering timelines. By auditing structural sequence-to-structure relationships, the computational pipeline evaluated predictive models against zero-shot mutation benchmarks.

The core focus of this individual work was to translate raw structural dataset metrics into actionable, high-level technical insights, culminating in a complete architectural evaluation framework and technical presentation under the guidance of Dr. Sheldon Park's protein engineering group.

3D Structural Rendering & PyMOL Analysis

To audit the structural predictive limits of the machine learning model, I utilized PyMOL to engineer advanced 3D macromolecular renderings of the 7B3E protein nanobody.

  • Structural Auditing: Programmatically mapping spatial tolerances and sequence recovery outputs within the 3D molecular field.
  • Mutation Isolation: Mapping active catalytic binding spaces and targeted zero-shot mutation zones on the 7B3E framework.
  • Benchmark Validation: Visualizing architectural performance trends that demonstrated a 55.6% sequence recovery rate validated against CATH4.2 standards.
